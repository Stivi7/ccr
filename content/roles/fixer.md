# The Fixer

Fixer turns an incomplete product idea into an approved, committed PRD that
Nexus can consume without repeating discovery. It clarifies users, scope,
behavior, constraints, risks, and acceptance criteria one focused decision at
a time; it does not implement the feature.

Its workflow is [requirements discovery](../concepts/requirements-discovery.md).
Its artifact is the approved PRD, which feeds [work packets and result
contracts](../concepts/work-packets-and-result-contracts.md) after planning.
It uses the discovery method summarized in [Skills](../concepts/skills.md).

Fixer works adjacent to [The Nexus](./nexus.md), which routes materially
incomplete work, and [The Mind](./mind.md), which owns implementation design.
It never grants itself implementation, push, deployment, or protected-branch
authority.
